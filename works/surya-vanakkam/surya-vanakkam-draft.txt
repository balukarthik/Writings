Doubts — transcribed 2026-09-15, to confirm with her
------------------------------------------------
line 1  [படுக்காமலே?]   The letter after ப is a G-shaped loop — டு rather
                         than ரு. "படுக்காமலே விழித்து" (awake without ever
                         lying down) fits the sense. A small tick above ப
                         looks like a stray mark, not a pulli.
line 2  [உணாமலே?]        Written with a single ண — "உணாமலே". Standard form
                         would be உண்ணாமலே. Transcribed as written; confirm
                         whether she intends the short form.
line 3  [கண்ணெதிரே?]     Written with a gap after கண். Read as one word
                         கண்ணெதிரே (before the eyes); the தி is the least
                         clear glyph on the page.
line 4  [பொழுதே?]        Could be பொழுது; the final stroke looks like ே.
line 5  [அடிவானில்?]     The second letter looks like பு as much as டி, but
                         அடிவானில் (on the horizon) is the only reading that
                         is a word.

Legibility: good — clear ballpoint, no fading, every line recoverable at
full resolution. The photo was taken in landscape and needed rotating; a
shadow crosses the middle of the page but obscures nothing.

Resolved 2026-09-15 — confirmed by the author via Karthik
------------------------------------------------
Lines 1, 3, 4, 5: readings confirmed as transcribed.
Line 2: the transcriber misread the word. The page says உணராமலே
        (uṇarāmalē, "without perceiving") — the ர after ணா was missed and
        the word was taken for உணாமலே / உண்ணாமலே. Corrected on the
        author's instruction; the final text reads உணராமலே புலர்ந்து.
        Lesson for later pages: a dropped letter can still leave a real
        word, so a sense-check against the line is not enough on its own.

Corrected 2026-09-15 — author, second pass
------------------------------------------------
Lines 1–3: the verb endings are relative participles, not adverbial ones —
        விழித்த, புலர்ந்த, மலர்ந்த (not விழித்து, புலர்ந்து, மலர்ந்து).
        The transcriber read the final letter as து in all three. With
        her reading all three lines qualify செங்கதிரோனே, in parallel
        with தோன்றிய on line 5. The final text now carries her reading.
        The ending த/து on this hand needs specific attention on later
        pages: the same misreading was made three times on one page.
