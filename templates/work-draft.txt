WORKING DRAFT — not the archive copy. The clean text lives in {{SLUG}}.txt.

Keep this file. It is the audit trail: it records which notebook page every
line came from, and which readings were ever in doubt.

Conventions
-----------

[margin: …] an insertion from the margin, placed where it belongs in the text
[note: …]  something on the page that is not part of the work (a date, a list)
